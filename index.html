Our Planet in Peril

You cannot solve a crisis without understanding it first.

🌡️

Global Warming

Glaciers are melting, and sea levels are rising. Extreme weather will become the new normal if we don't control carbon emissions.

🌊

Ocean Pollution

8 million tons of plastic enter our oceans every year. By 2050, there could be more plastic than fish in the sea.

🌳

Biodiversity Loss

Deforestation destroys habitats daily. We are currently amidst the sixth mass extinction event.

What Can You Do?

Change begins with small habits.

♻️ Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

Sort your waste carefully, say no to single-use plastics, and recycle resources whenever possible.

🚲 Low-Carbon Travel

Walk or cycle for short distances. Choose public transport for longer trips to reduce your carbon footprint.

💡 Save Energy

Turn off lights when leaving a room, use energy-efficient appliances, and manage your heating/cooling wisely.

🥗 Eat Green

Reduce food waste, try a plant-based diet more often, and support local, organic produce.
